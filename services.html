What We Offer

Beyond Beautiful Jewellery

We don't just sell jewellery — we offer a complete luxury experience designed around your most precious moments.

01

Custom Design Studio

Co-create your dream piece with our master designers. From sketch to 3D CAD to finished masterwork — entirely bespoke, entirely yours. Typically 3–6 weeks from concept to delivery.

Book a Session
02

Bridal Consultation

A dedicated bridal specialist guides you through the full trousseau — matching necklaces, earrings, maang tikka, bangles, and nose rings for a cohesive, breathtaking look.

Book Consultation
03

Gemstone Certification

Independent GIA, IGI, and HRD certification for every diamond and precious gemstone. Full grading reports on cut, colour, clarity, and carat provided at no extra charge.

Learn More
04

Lifetime Buyback

Exchange or encash any Royale piece at fair prevailing market rates — no questions, no hassle. Your jewellery is an investment that holds its value across generations.

View Policy
05

Insured Home Delivery

Free, fully insured home delivery across India in tamper-proof, temperature-controlled packaging with real-time GPS tracking and signature-on-delivery.

Get in Touch
06

Repair & Restoration

Expert repair, resizing, re-polishing, and full restoration of heirloom pieces. Breathing new life into cherished family jewellery is one of our most deeply satisfying services.

Book Repair
07

Virtual Consultation

Live video consultations with our jewellery experts — browse pieces in real-time via our HD studio cameras, ask questions, and make confident decisions from your home.

Book Virtual Call
08

Corporate Gifting

Luxury jewellery gifts for employees, clients, and milestones. Bulk orders with custom engraving, branded packaging, and personalised gift notes — managed end-to-end.

Get a Quote
09

Jewellery Appraisal

Certified appraisal services for insurance, estate planning, or resale. Conducted by our in-house GIA-trained gemologists with full written documentation.

Book Appraisal
