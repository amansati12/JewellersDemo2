Gold & Purity
BIS Hallmarking is a mandatory quality certification by the Bureau of Indian Standards. It guarantees that your gold jewellery meets the purity level stamped on it — 916 means 22 karat (91.6% pure gold), 750 means 18 karat (75% pure gold). At Royale, every single gold piece is BIS Hallmarked without exception, giving you complete statutory protection as a buyer.
22 karat gold (916 purity) is 91.6% pure gold, making it richer in colour and more valuable by weight — ideal for traditional Indian jewellery, bangles, and haaars. 18 karat gold (750 purity) is 75% pure and alloyed with harder metals, making it more durable and suitable for diamond-set jewellery and daily-wear pieces. Both are hallmarked and certified at Royale.
Gold prices fluctuate daily based on international commodity markets and currency exchange rates. Our jewellery is priced based on the prevailing gold rate on the day of purchase plus making charges. We always display the current gold rate prominently in our showrooms and are fully transparent about how your piece is priced. Our lifetime buyback policy is also linked to the prevailing market rate at the time of exchange.
Diamond Certification
GIA (Gemological Institute of America) is the world's most respected independent diamond grading laboratory. A GIA certificate is a detailed report that independently verifies a diamond's Cut, Colour, Clarity, and Carat weight — the 4Cs. It is not issued by us; it is an independent third-party assessment. Every diamond above 0.30 carats at Royale comes with a full GIA report, so you know exactly what you're buying.
Yes, we offer a curated selection of IGI-certified lab-grown diamonds — chemically, physically, and optically identical to mined diamonds, but available at significantly lower price points. Lab-grown diamonds are clearly labelled and priced separately from natural diamonds. Whether you prefer natural or lab-grown, both receive the same craftsmanship and certification standards at Royale.

Delivery & Returns
All home deliveries are fully insured for the declared value of the piece. Jewellery is shipped in tamper-evident, temperature-controlled packaging via our dedicated logistics partner. You receive real-time GPS tracking and the delivery is signature-on-arrival only. Our delivery team is trained and uniformed. We offer free insured delivery across all major cities in India.
We offer a 15-day return policy on standard catalogue pieces (unused and in original condition with all original packaging and certificates). Custom-designed pieces are non-returnable but can be exchanged for full value. Beyond 15 days, our lifetime buyback policy applies — you may exchange or encash any Royale piece at fair prevailing market rates at any of our showrooms.
Custom Design
Our custom design journey begins with a free consultation — in-showroom or virtual — where you share your vision, references, occasion, and budget. Our designers then create hand sketches and 3D CAD renderings for your review and approval. Once approved, our master artisans craft the piece by hand over 3–6 weeks. You can visit at any stage to check progress. The finished piece is delivered with full certification and a Royale heritage box.
Our custom design service starts from ₹25,000 for simple single-piece designs. For complex bridal sets or multi-piece commissions, budgets typically range from ₹2 lakhs upward. There is no upper limit — some of our most extraordinary commissions have been family heirloom projects spanning ₹50 lakhs and above. Book a consultation and we'll guide you honestly on what's achievable within your budget.
Care & Maintenance
The safest home cleaning method is warm (not hot) water with a few drops of mild dish soap. Gently scrub with a soft toothbrush, rinse thoroughly with clean water, and pat dry with a lint-free cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals, bleach, or ultrasonic cleaners for antique or kundan pieces. We recommend a professional polish at any Royale showroom once a year — this service is complimentary for Royale purchases.
Yes. All pieces purchased at Royale are eligible for one complimentary annual professional cleaning and polish at any of our our showroom. Prong checking for diamond-set pieces is also included free of charge. Additional repairs (prong retipping, clasp replacement, sizing) are charged at cost with full transparency and a written estimate before any work begins.
