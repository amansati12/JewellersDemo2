Our Trusted Partners

Global Alliances

Partnerships Built on Prestige

Royale collaborates with the world's most respected gemological institutes, certification bodies, and luxury associations — because your trust deserves the world's best guarantors.

Certification & Standards

Official Certifying Bodies

Premium
GIA
Gemological Institute of America
Diamond Certification

The world's foremost authority on diamonds, coloured stones, and pearls. Every Royale diamond carries a full GIA grading report covering the 4Cs.

IGI
International Gemological Institute
Diamond & Lab Diamond Grading

IGI-certified diamonds are independently assessed for cut, colour, clarity, and carat weight — your assurance of authenticity and fair value.

BIS
Bureau of Indian Standards
Gold Hallmarking

Mandatory BIS Hallmarking on all our gold jewellery — statutory protection for Indian consumers, guaranteed on every single piece we create.

HRD
HRD Antwerp
European Diamond Certification

Established in 1976 in Antwerp's legendary diamond district, HRD certification is globally recognised as the European gold standard of diamond quality.

WGC
World Gold Council
Gold Industry Authority

WGC membership keeps us at the forefront of global gold market intelligence, design trends, and responsible sourcing practices.

Exclusive
FM
Forevermark by De Beers
Premium Diamond Partnership

Authorised Forevermark retailer — offering De Beers' finest diamonds that are responsibly sourced, natural, untreated, and inscribed with a unique ID.

Luxury Alliances

Brand & Industry Partners

PGI
Platinum Guild India
Platinum Jewellery

Authorised Platinum Guild retailer — pure Pt 950 jewellery endorsed and certified by Platinum Guild International, London.

GJEPC
GJEPC India
Export Promotion Council

Member of the Gem & Jewellery Export Promotion Council, ensuring our quality standards meet international benchmarks.

SW
Swarovski Elements
Crystal Embellishments

Select collections feature genuine Swarovski crystals — the world's finest precision-cut crystals for signature sparkle.

GIA
GIA Alumni Network
Education & Training

Our entire gemology team is GIA-educated — ongoing training ensures the sharpest expert knowledge in the industry.
