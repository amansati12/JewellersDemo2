Our Story &

29

The Beginning

Born from a Passion
for Perfection

In 1995, the founder of Gokul Jewellers established our first showroom on Kirti Mandir Road, Porbandar — bringing together master craftsmanship and a deep passion for unique, custom jewellery.

He believed that jewellery was not mere ornament but the physical expression of the most tender human emotions — love, devotion, celebration, memory. Every piece he crafted carried that belief.

Four decades later, Gokul Jewellers is a name whispered with reverence at engagements, cherished at weddings, and passed as heirlooms to grandchildren — a legacy made of gold, diamonds, and unwavering integrity.

Our Journey

Milestones of Excellence

1995
The First Atelier

Gokul Founder opens a small workshop in Porbandar, Gujarat. First week sees 3 orders; all three families still shop with us today.

1991
First Flagship Store

Expanded from workshop to a 3,000 sq. ft. showroom — the first in India to display jewellery under gallery-style lighting.

1998
GIA Partnership

Became India's first GIA-partnered independent jeweller, setting the gold standard for diamond certification and consumer transparency.

2003
National Excellence Award

Received the National Jewellery Design Excellence Award from the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India.

2008
Custom Design Studio

Launched India's first jewellery custom-design studio where clients co-create pieces with master designers using 3D CAD technology.

2014
Platinum Guild Alliance

Exclusive authorised partner for Platinum Guild India, bringing world-class platinum jewellery to discerning Indian connoisseurs.

2019
Pan-India Expansion

Reached our showroom across 8 cities. Introduced insured home delivery and virtual consultation — a first for Indian fine jewellery.

2025
Our Showroom & Beyond

75,000+ families, skilled artisans, 35,000+ unique designs. The journey continues — stronger, more radiant, and ever more devoted to craft.

Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Values

01

Our Mission

To craft jewellery that is an enduring symbol of love, trust, and artistry — making every milestone of life shine brighter. We are committed to unmatched quality, ethical sourcing, and personalised service that makes every customer feel truly valued.

02

Our Vision

To be India's most beloved fine jewellery brand — a name that resonates across every household as a beacon of trust, beauty, and cultural richness. We envision jewellery that bridges tradition and modernity, heritage and bold innovation.

03

Our Values

Integrity in every hallmark. Transparency in every transaction. Artistry in every detail. Empathy in every conversation. These are not slogans — they are the daily practice of 1,200 hands who craft with conscience.
